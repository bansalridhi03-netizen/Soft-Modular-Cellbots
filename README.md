# Soft Scalable Self-Reconfigurable Modular Cellbots

## Project Overview

This project was undertaken as part of my PhD research in Robotics and Autonomous Systems at the University of Bristol and Bristol Robotics Laboratory.

The research focuses on developing bio-inspired soft modular robots capable of locomotion, reconfiguration, and adapting to different environmental conditions.

## Research Areas

- Mechanical design and fabrication of soft robotic modules
- 3D modelling and prototyping
- Pneumatic actuation and control
- Robot locomotion and self-reconfiguration
- MATLAB, Simulink, and Simscape Multibody simulations
- Experimental investigation of friction and actuator forces
- Modular robotic configurations and locomotion strategies
